What a day looks like

Morning

You put the intention down and read it back. A few minutes, before the day has an opinion about it.

Evening

You write what actually happened. Some nights that is a receipt. Most nights it is just the practice.

Where your writing goes

What you write is kept on your phone. There is no account to sign up for and no server holding your journal.

Some features send the text of an intention to an AI model so it can write something back to you. The privacy policy names which model that is, what is sent, and what happens to it afterwards.
